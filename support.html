How Can You Help Out?

We need shoes:

Donations of brand new, brand name shoes in all sizes for school aged kids in Shamattawa from Kindergarten all the way to Grade 12.

Host a shoe party:

This involves no giving on your part, only getting! Whether it's a birthday party or a housewarming party, simply let your guests know that instead of bringing you a gift, they either make a donation to The Sidney Project or bring a pair of shoes to the party for The Sidney Project.

We need money:

(to buy more shoes)


Don't feel like you have the time to shop for shoes? Just etransfer thesidneyproject@yahoo.com and we'll make sure every penny goes towards the purchase of shoes.

Our mission is to increase the overall well-being of students in Shamattawa by providing as many students as we can with a brand new, brand name pair of running shoes. We hope that to own a pair of indoor shoes will mean more physical activity and socialization thus increasing their overall happiness.

Making days a little brighter and lives a little better, two shoes at a time
